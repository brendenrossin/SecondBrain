Why SecondBrain?

Your notes are valuable. Most of that value is locked away.

  • Semantic search across your entire vault — not just keyword matching
  • Morning briefings that surface what matters today
  • Ingest external content (articles, YouTube, PDFs) into structured wiki pages
  • AI safety auditor reviews everything before it touches your vault
  • All data stays on your machine — vault is the single source of truth

Capture & Knowledge Library

Quick text capture URL ingestion (articles) YouTube transcript extraction PDF parsing AI safety auditor gates all content Wiki compiler → structured vault pages
## OBS Capture Instructions: capture-url.gif **Duration:** ~15-20 seconds (longer — this is the wow feature) **Window:** Browser at /capture **What to record:** 1. Start on the capture page 2. Paste a URL of an interesting article or YouTube video - Good examples: a blog post about AI, a tech talk transcript 3. Click submit / capture 4. Show the processing state (safety audit running, then wiki compilation) 5. If there's a "Save as Wiki" chip, click it to show the compiled output 6. Optionally: show the resulting wiki page in the vault **Tips:** - Pick a URL that processes in under 10 seconds - The safety audit step is a key differentiator — highlight it - If recording YouTube, pick a short video (~5 min) for fast transcript extraction - This is the Knowledge Library (KLIB-1) feature — the Karpathy-style content ingestion
