## Summary

The Sunday meal-prep workhorse. One pan, ~45 min, feeds me 3 nights.

## Ingredients

- 6 chicken thighs, bone-in skin-on
- 1.5 lb baby potatoes, halved
- 1 lemon, sliced into rounds
- 6 cloves garlic, smashed
- 2 sprigs rosemary (or oregano)
- Olive oil, salt, pepper, paprika
- Optional: cherry tomatoes added last 15 min

## Method

1. Heat oven 425°F.
2. Toss potatoes + garlic + olive oil + salt on a sheet pan. Roast 15 min.
3. Pat chicken dry, season heavily. Push potatoes to the side, add chicken skin-up. Tuck lemon slices and rosemary around.
4. Roast 30–35 min until chicken hits 165°F and skin is crisp.
5. Rest 5 min, scoop pan juices over everything.

## Notes

- Skin-on bone-in is non-negotiable. Boneless gets sad.
- 2026-05-04: Doubled the recipe, used two pans. Lasted me through Thursday lunch.
- Leftovers shred well — taco filling, salad topper, sandwich.
