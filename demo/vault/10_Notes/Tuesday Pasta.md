## Summary

My default weeknight pasta. 25 min from "I'm hungry" to plate. Variations below.

## Base

- 200g pasta (orecchiette or rigatoni)
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 4 cloves garlic, sliced
- Red pepper flakes
- Splash of pasta water
- Pecorino, lots of black pepper

## Method

1. Salt water heavily. Boil pasta to 1 min under package time.
2. While pasta cooks: olive oil + garlic + flakes in cold pan, medium heat. Garlic should go pale gold, not brown.
3. Reserve a cup of pasta water before draining.
4. Dump pasta into the garlic pan, splash of water, toss hard. Off heat, finish with pecorino + pepper.

## Variations

- **+ broccoli rabe** (blanch with the pasta last 3 min)
- **+ anchovies** (melt 4 into the oil — adds salty depth, no fish flavor)
- **+ tinned sardines** (controversial, actually great)
- **+ peas + lemon zest** (spring version)
