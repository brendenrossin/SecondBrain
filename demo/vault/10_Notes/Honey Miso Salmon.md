## Summary

Quick weeknight salmon, broiler-finished. The miso-honey glaze is the whole point.

## Ingredients (serves 2)

- 2 salmon fillets, skin-on (~6 oz each)
- 2 tbsp white miso
- 1 tbsp honey (next time: try 1/2 tbsp — too sweet)
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp grated ginger
- 1 tsp rice vinegar
- 1 scallion, sliced thin
- Sesame seeds

## Method

1. Whisk miso, honey, soy, ginger, vinegar. Reserve a spoonful for finishing.
2. Pat salmon dry. Brush glaze on top side.
3. Broil on high, 7–8 min, no flipping. Glaze should bubble and darken in spots.
4. Drizzle reserved glaze, top with scallion + sesame.

## Notes

- Serve with rice and a quick cucumber salad (rice vinegar + sesame oil + salt).
- 2026-05-15: First attempt — sauce too sweet. Halve the honey next time. Could also lean miso harder.
