## Summary

Running notes on my sourdough loaves. Starter is "Vinnie." Vinnie is fine.

## Current recipe (after ~30 loaves of iteration)

- 500g bread flour
- 50g whole wheat
- 375g water (75% hydration)
- 100g active starter
- 11g salt

## Schedule

- 8am: feed starter
- 1pm: starter peaked, mix dough (autolyse 30 min first)
- 1:30: add salt, do 4 stretch-and-folds 30 min apart
- 6pm: shape, bench rest 30 min, into banneton, fridge overnight
- Next morning: bake from cold — 500°F covered 20 min, 450°F uncovered 22 min

## Notes

- **The single biggest variable is starter health.** Vinnie peaks at 4–5 hours after feeding at room temp.
- 2026-04-19: tried 80% hydration. Crumb was great, shaping was hell. Going back to 75%.
- 2026-05-09: most consistent loaf in weeks. Ear is finally what I wanted. Score 30° angle, deep cut.
- Lazy bake (warm oven only): also fine for sandwich bread. Doesn't matter for sandwiches.

## Open questions

- Does fridge retard length matter? I've done 8h and 18h and tasted no difference. Need to actually A/B.
- Worth doing 50/50 whole wheat? Sam swears by it.
